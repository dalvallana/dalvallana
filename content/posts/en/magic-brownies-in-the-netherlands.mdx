Spain has a very generous long weekend at the beginning of December. On the 6th, 7th and 8th there are no classes at any university in Madrid or anywhere else in Spain, so I decided to take a trip with some Venezuelan friends to the land of tulips and marijuana (not legal, but tolerated).

We got to see Amsterdam, Delft, Rotterdam and The Hague. The first thing that was hard to ignore was how much snow there was and how cold it was. It had snowed a ton those days and, compared with Madrid, the cold was unbearable. Even so, we enjoyed the trip a lot. We even got to do a few light drugs, and we explored Amsterdam's Red Light District as if it were the most interesting museum in Europe.

Compared with Spain, the Netherlands is not the ideal country for going out for drinks every day and having some tapas for a change. It is very expensive; by comparison, I repeat. Still, it never hurts to escape the cold of the street and step into a warm bar in the center of Amsterdam for a dark beer that comforts you and recharges you to carry on exploring the city on foot.

Since we had come to discover new places and cultures, we kept an open mind about walking into a coffee shop, the typical places where you can get marijuana and brownies that make you laugh. So we did, and we bought our share of joints and brownies. All I can say about it is that the joints feel good at first, but if you are not used to them, the nausea can be terrible. The brownies are another matter. I liked them so much that I bought one for every day I was in the Netherlands. The feeling is not as intense, but it is enough to make you laugh at the stupidest joke one of your friends can come up with. One side effect of the brownies is the ferocious hunger that hits you when the effect wears off. I remember raiding a friend's fridge to steal some cold pasta with no sauce at all and eating it with my hands. I don't remember enjoying a meal that much all year.

We also got to see the famous magic mushrooms, but none of us had the balls to try them. We have seen enough videos of their effects on YouTube to have a healthy respect for them.

Another place worth mentioning in the Netherlands (in Amsterdam, to be precise) is the Red Light District. There was something for everyone and every taste. I have to single out one girl, whom we named Kari for her charisma (_carisma_ in Spanish), and whom we couldn't help watching in her window for three or four minutes. She kept beckoning us with her index finger to come and have a good time with her. When she saw we were shy, she started putting in a bit more effort and making thrusting movements that left us stunned. Not content with that, she grabbed a broom she had nearby and started miming oral sex with the handle. Now that's showing up to work with enthusiasm! None of us did anything; it's not our style. But we don't judge either.
